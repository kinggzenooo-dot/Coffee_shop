Espresso & Drink Standards Quick Guide

Official Daily Grind brewing parameters, steam temperatures, and pump counts.

☕ Signature Espresso Dose & Yield

Dose: 18.5g in • Yield: 37g out • Time: 26–29 seconds • Temp: 93.5°C

🥛 Milk Steaming Temperatures

Whole / 2%: 62°C–65°C • Oat / Almond: 58°C–60°C • Extra Hot: 72°C max

🍯 Flavor Syrup Standard Pumps

Small (12oz): 2 pumps • Medium (16oz): 3 pumps • Large (20oz): 4 pumps • Half Sweet: Cut in half

🍵 Ceremonial Matcha Latte Prep

Whisk: 3.5g Uji Matcha with 40ml 80°C water for 30s until frothy, then combine with steamed oat milk.
